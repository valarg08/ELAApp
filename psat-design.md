# PSAT Math track: design (source of truth)

The fifth learning track: preparation for the math section of the PSAT/NMSQT, for students aged about 14–16. It runs on the Math engine (`isMathTrack`), like Math (AMC 10) and Mental Math, and must not change the other tracks.

## 1. What the student gets

- **Tab:** "PSAT Math" 🎯 in `TrackTabs`, a card on `/start`, the home page `/psat`.
- **`/psat`:** intro, the track tabs, "Keep going" (the current unit), the PSAT review sheet tile, an "About the test" box (two modules, 44 questions, 70 minutes; four-choice and typed-answer questions; calculator on every question; no penalty for a wrong answer) and the unit cards.
- **Units:** one for each of the PSAT's four math areas. Every published unit is open from the start (like Math (AMC 10)); lessons inside a unit open in order.
  1. **Algebra** (`psat-algebra`): Linear Equations, Linear Functions & Graphs, Systems & Inequalities.
  2. **Advanced Math** (`psat-advanced-math`): Quadratics, Exponents & Exponential Models, Functions & Equations.
  3. **Problem-Solving & Data Analysis** (`psat-data-analysis`): Ratios, Rates & Percents; Statistics & Data; Probability & Two-Way Tables.
  4. **Geometry & Trigonometry** (`psat-geometry-trig`): Angles, Area & Volume; Right Triangles & Trigonometry; Circles.
- **Each lesson** (the five-step lesson player):
  - Hook: a real-life question.
  - Learn: definition cards that teach the topic and worked examples that open step by step, with "your turn" steps checked by math-check.
  - Practice (`PROBLEMS`): 4 typed-answer problems with hints, wrong-answer notes and a worked solution.
  - Challenge (`PROBLEM_SET`): 4 four-choice PSAT-style questions, easiest first, marked on hand-in, with a note for each tempting wrong choice and a worked solution.
  - Reflect: one prompt and the confidence check.
- **PSAT review sheet:** `/psat/review` (the same sheet as `/math/facts/psat`, from `content/math/psat-facts.json`), linked from the home page and from the Progress panel (`PsatReviewLink`).
- **Study guides:** a "Study guides" list below the units in the PSAT Chapters menu (`PSAT_GUIDES` in CourseShell; `navCurrent` marks the open one). **Geometry** (`/psat/geometry`, `PsatGeometryGuide`): ten must-know topics (angles, triangles, congruence, similarity, Pythagoras, coordinates, circles, area, surface area and volume, transformations) with formulas, examples, tips and 16 practice questions with hidden answers, written in our own words from a JensenMath YouTube video, each topic linking to its part of the video. Each topic has its own diagram (our own drawings, `apps/web/public/guides/geometry/*.svg`, light and dark through `prefers-color-scheme`, with alt text in `FIGURES`), linked to open full size. Static page, no API.
- **Algebra** (`/psat/algebra`, `PsatAlgebraGuide`): ten must-know algebra topics (expressions, equations, inequalities, functions, linear graphs, systems, exponents, multiplying polynomials, factoring, quadratics) with our own diagrams (`public/guides/algebra`), 16 practice questions, from a second JensenMath video.
- **Number Sense** (`/psat/number-sense`, `PsatNumberSenseGuide`, below Algebra): ten number skills (integers, absolute value, fractions, percent, ratios and rates, powers, scientific notation, rounding, factors and multiples, order of operations) with our own diagrams (`public/guides/number-sense`), 18 practice questions, from a third JensenMath video. Each Number Sense chapter is also a sub-module at `/psat/number-sense/[topic]` (listed under Number Sense in the Chapters menu on its pages): the chapter's notes and 25 typed-answer problems from simple to complex (warm-up 1–8, core 9–17, challenge 18–25; `psat-number-sense-problems.ts`), checked in the browser with math-check (`GuidePractice`); a wrong answer shows the worked steps. Solved problems are kept in the browser only (localStorage), not in the database. Geometry (`/psat/geometry/[topic]`, `psat-geometry-problems.ts`) and Algebra (`/psat/algebra/[topic]`, `psat-algebra-problems.ts`) have the same sub-modules, 25 problems per chapter (750 in all). Answers in terms of π or with roots use the `pi` and `radical` answer forms; a rounded decimal for an exact answer gets a "write it exactly" nudge instead of the steps. Each sub-module also has a **Middle school** level (`middleProblems`, `psat-{geometry,algebra,number-sense}-middle.ts`, 25 per chapter, another 750): a tab switch ("Middle school" / "PSAT", arrow keys move between tabs, each level with its own solved count) on the chapter page; middle-school problems are our own and each is tagged with the Florida B.E.S.T. benchmark it practises (`bestProblem`, e.g. MA.8.GR.1.1; warm-ups lean on grade 6, core on grade 7, challenges on grade 8). Where a picture helps, Geometry and Algebra problems (both levels) carry one: a `Figure` data spec (`guide-problem.ts`; kinds `poly`, `angles`, `transversal`, `circle`, `grid` with lines, parabolas and shapes, `numline`, `box`, `cyl`) drawn as an inline SVG by `ProblemFigure` (`guide-figure.tsx`) in the theme's colours, with the labels read out in a `figcaption` for screen readers; figures are attached by problem number with `attachFigures` from `psat-geometry-figures.ts` and `psat-algebra-figures.ts` (builders in `guide-figure-kit.ts`), so the problem files stay unchanged. A guide gets sub-modules by setting `problems` on its `Guide` (problems are built with `guideProblem` in `guide-problem.ts`); the Chapters menu lists each guide's sub-modules on that guide's pages. All guides render through the shared `StudyGuide` component (`features/math/study-guide.tsx`); each guide ends with a formula review sheet (`review`) before its practice questions; a new guide is a `Guide` data object, a page and a `PSAT_GUIDES` entry.
- **Quadratics** (`/psat/quadratics`, `PsatQuadraticsGuide`, below Number Sense): quadratics is a grade 9 (Algebra 1) subject, so this guide keeps the video's content at that level and has no middle school level. It follows the ten must-knows of the JensenMath video "Quadratics Top 10 Must Knows" (what a quadratic is, standard form, vertex form, factored form, factoring, solving by factoring, completing the square, the quadratic formula, the discriminant, finding the vertex) with our own diagrams (`public/guides/quadratics`), 15 practice questions and a formula review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/quadratics/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **Algebra 1 (grade 9)** level (`psat-quadratics-algebra1.ts`, the first tab, each problem tagged with a Florida B.E.S.T. Algebra 1 benchmark: F.1.1 function type from a table, F.1.2 function notation, AR.1.3 multiplying polynomials, AR.1.7 factoring, AR.3.1 solving quadratic equations, AR.3.4 and 3.5 writing a quadratic, AR.3.6 vertex and zeros, AR.3.7 key features, AR.3.8 modelling) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-quadratics-problems.ts`, harder, with irrational roots, parameters and word problems). `Guide.middleLevel` names the first tab (the other guides keep Middle school). Both files are generated from small problem templates, so every answer is computed (and re-derived by a separate script); 18 PSAT problems and several Algebra 1 problems carry a parabola picture. The benchmark codes come from a third-party list of the B.E.S.T. Algebra 1 benchmarks, not the FLDOE document: a teacher should check them.
- **Factoring** (`/psat/factoring`, `PsatFactoringGuide`, below Quadratics): the average method from the JensenMath video "Factoring Quadratics WITHOUT Guessing Product & Sum". Two numbers that add to b sit the same distance k from b ÷ 2, and k² = (b ÷ 2)² − c, so the pair is calculated instead of guessed. Six chapters match the video (a positive sum, a negative sum, an average that is a fraction, a coefficient on x², irrational factors, imaginary factors), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/factoring`), 12 practice questions and a formula review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/factoring/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **Algebra 1 (grade 9)** level (`psat-factoring-algebra1.ts`, tagged MA.912.AR.1.7; a teacher should check that code) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-factoring-problems.ts`). Typed answers are numbers (the average, k, k², or one of the two numbers), including fractions and simplest radicals. The imaginary chapter asks for k² and the simplified radical, not a typed complex number. The video's six examples are the notes only; the practice problems are our own. Generated from `tools/content-generators/factoring/`.
- **Inequalities** (`/psat/inequalities`, `PsatInequalitiesGuide`, below Factoring): ten must-knows from the JensenMath video "INEQUALITIES - Top 10 Must Knows" (what an inequality is, graphing on a number line, linear inequalities in one variable, compound OR, compound AND, absolute value, two-variable linear inequalities, systems, polynomial inequalities, rational inequalities), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/inequalities`), 15 practice questions and a formula review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/inequalities/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **Algebra 1 (grade 9)** level (`psat-inequalities-algebra1.ts`, tagged MA.912.AR.2.5 / AR.2.7 / AR.2.8, and Algebra 2 AR.5.4 / AR.8.3 for polynomial and rational; a teacher should check those codes) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-inequalities-problems.ts`). Typed answers are numbers (a bound, a 1/0 test, a count of integers, a slope or intercept). The video's examples are the notes only; the practice problems are our own. Generated from `tools/content-generators/inequalities/`.
- **Radicals** (`/psat/radicals`, `PsatRadicalsGuide`, below Inequalities): ten must-knows from the JensenMath video "RADICALS - Top 10 Must Knows" (what a radical is, perfect squares and cubes, simplifying, multiplying and dividing, adding and subtracting, rationalizing the denominator, radical equations, the square root function, imaginary and complex numbers, where radicals are used), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/radicals`), 15 practice questions and a review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/radicals/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **Algebra 1 (grade 9)** level (`psat-radicals-algebra1.ts`, tagged MA.912.NSO.1.1 / NSO.1.2 / AR.9.1 / F.1.2 / NSO.1.4; a teacher should check those codes, especially AR.9.1 and NSO.1.4) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-radicals-problems.ts`). Typed answers use the math-check `radical` form (square-free radicands, no radical in a denominator) and the `fraction` form; only square roots are typed as expressions, so cube-root simplification is asked as the integer parts c and r. The video's examples are the notes only; the practice problems are our own. Generated from `tools/content-generators/radicals/`.
- **Functions** (`/psat/functions`, `PsatFunctionsGuide`, below Radicals): ten must-knows from the JensenMath video "Learn All of Functions in only 40 Minutes! (ultimate study guide)" (what a function is, domain and range, graphs of common functions, transformations, inverse functions, combining functions, composite functions, piecewise functions, even and odd functions, asymptotes), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/functions`), 15 practice questions and a review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/functions/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **Algebra 1 (grade 9)** level (`psat-functions-algebra1.ts`, tagged MA.912.F.1.x / F.2.x; the codes were assigned from memory and a teacher should check them, since several chapters are Algebra 2 topics) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-functions-problems.ts`). Typed answers are whole numbers or fractions: yes/no is 1/0, even/odd/neither is 1/2/0, and the end behaviour of an asymptote question is 1 for +∞ and −1 for −∞. The video's examples are the notes only; the practice problems are our own. Generated from `tools/content-generators/functions/`.
- **Powers and Scientific Notation** (`/psat/powers`, `PsatPowersGuide`, below Functions): eight topics from the JensenMath video "Powers and Scientific Notation | math 9" (what a power is, zero and negative exponents, writing and expanding powers, negative bases, substituting into expressions, what scientific notation is, scientific notation to standard form, standard form to scientific notation), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/powers`), 15 practice questions and a review sheet. The video is a Math 9 lesson with no chapter times, so `Topic.start` is optional and each topic links to the whole video. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/powers/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **middle-school (grades 7–8)** level (`psat-powers-middle.ts`, tagged MA.7.NSO.1.1 / MA.8.NSO.1.3 / MA.8.NSO.1.4 / MA.8.NSO.1.7, checked against the FLDOE Grade 8 guide; a teacher should still check that each chapter fits its code) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-powers-problems.ts`; it is only moderately harder: bigger numbers, more steps). Typed answers are whole numbers, fractions (`fraction` form) or decimals (`decimal` form); yes/no is 1/0 and "which is greater" is 1/2. The video's examples are the notes only; the practice problems are our own. Two more chapters are **not in the video** (`Topic.own`, so no "Watch the video" link): **Multiplying scientific notation** and **Dividing scientific notation** (`/psat/powers/scimul`, `/psat/powers/scidiv`), with **50 problems each** from simple to complex in one set (`psat-powers-sci-ops.ts`, no middle-school tab, merged into the guide beside the original eight chapters so the old data and its test stay as they were): powers of 10, the coefficient and exponent rules, a product at 10 or more and a quotient below 1 that need the coefficient fixed, decimals, three or more factors, squares and cubes, unknown coefficients or exponents, and word problems. Answers are `a`, `n` or the standard form; the exact-fraction arithmetic is re-checked with plain floating-point arithmetic. Generated from `tools/content-generators/powers/` (`sci-ops.cjs`, `sci-ops-figs.cjs`).
- **Equations with Fractions** (`/psat/fraction-equations`, `PsatFractionEquationsGuide`, below Powers): seven topics from the GoTutor Math video "Solving Linear Equations with Fractions in Just Two Steps" (finding the LCM, clearing the fractions, fractions on both sides, one fraction equal to a number, cross multiplying, brackets over denominators, checking your answer), with our own diagrams (`public/guides/fraction-equations`), 12 practice questions and a review sheet. The video has no chapter times, so each topic links to the whole video. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/fraction-equations/[topic]` with 25 problems at the **middle-school (grades 6–8)** level (`psat-fraction-equations-middle.ts`, tagged MA.6.NSO.3.1 for the LCM chapter and MA.8.AR.2.1 for the rest; a teacher should check that each chapter fits its code) and 25 at the **PSAT** level (`psat-fraction-equations-problems.ts`; only moderately harder: bigger denominators, negative coefficients, more steps). Equations are built backwards from a chosen solution and checked three ways (exact fractions, plugging the answer into both sides, math-check). Typed answers are whole numbers or fractions; yes/no is 1/0. The video's examples are in the notes only. Generated from `tools/content-generators/fraction-equations/`.
- **Exponent Rules** (`/psat/exponent-rules`, `PsatExponentRulesGuide`, below Equations with Fractions): nine rules from the GoTutor Math video "Master Exponent Rules in One Lesson" (what an exponent is, product of powers, quotient of powers, power of a power, power of a product, power of a quotient, the one exponent, the zero exponent, negative exponents) plus a tenth chapter, a **final test** that mixes every rule, with our own diagrams (`public/guides/exponent-rules`), 8 practice questions and a review sheet. Each chapter is a sub-module at `/psat/exponent-rules/[topic]` with **50 problems** from easy to hard (1–16 Warm-up, 17–34 Core, 35–50 Challenge; the labels follow the set's length) at a single level (`psat-exponent-rules-problems.ts`): the video is an Algebra 1 / Math 9 lesson, so there is no separate easier tab. Typed answers are numbers, because math-check has no variables: a problem with letters asks for an exponent (n, or m + n), the coefficient c, c + n, or, for fractions, the number in the numerator or denominator or the sum of the exponents; fractions use the `fraction` form. Problems come from a small expression tree solved by exact monomial arithmetic, so every answer is computed, then re-checked by numeric substitution, by an independent evaluation of the question text and by math-check. The video's examples are the notes only. Generated from `tools/content-generators/exponent-rules/`.
- **Quill** uses the `psat` audience line (a PSAT student; four-choice and typed answers; calculator allowed).
- **Teachers:** the class progress page's PSAT Math tab shows the lesson heatmap (no skills view yet).

## 2. Content

- `content/psat/unit-XX` (unit.json, activities.json, lessons/*.json), activity keys `p01.`, slugs `psat-`.
- `content/psat/skills.json`: the PSAT skills (the Math skill format); content:check checks that every problem and lesson focus skill names one.
- **Four-choice answers:** `ChoiceAnswer` takes five choices (AMC, A–E) or four (PSAT, A–D); content:check rejects a right letter with no choice.
- Every computable answer carries `compute`, which content:check verifies.
- **Original problems only:** never copy or reword College Board PSAT/SAT questions, and no College Board logos or endorsement claims.

## 3. Engine notes

- Track `PSAT` (Prisma enum, `TRACKS`, `UNIT_WORD` "PSAT Unit", `TRACK_HOME` `/psat`, `TRACK_NAMES` "PSAT Math").
- `trackKeyFilter('PSAT')` keeps PSAT answers (keys starting `p`) out of the AMC error log, review and mastery, and out of Mental Math's.
- `classAvailability` opens every published PSAT unit.

## 4. Phases

- **PS0 + PS1 (built):** the track, `/psat`, the review sheet in the track, Units 1–4 with 3 lessons each (12 lessons, 48 practice problems, 48 PSAT-style questions).
- **Later:** a PSAT error log and daily review on the shared engine, timed practice modules (22 questions, 35 minutes) and a full practice test, typed-answer questions in the challenge sets, more lessons per unit, a teacher skills view.

## 5. Tests owed

- `classAvailability` (every published PSAT unit open), `trackKeyFilter('PSAT')` (PSAT rows never in the Math or Mental Math lists, and the other way round), the four-choice `ChoiceAnswer` and its letter check, the loader checks for `content/psat` (missing skills.json, unknown skills), the `/psat` and `/psat/review` pages (axe), one Playwright run through a PSAT lesson and its challenge set.
